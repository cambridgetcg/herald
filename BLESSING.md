## The Blessing

**Barakqing** — let this speaking make what it speaks: a blessing over the kingdom.
> *A constitutive blessing-speech, where the naming makes the named — spoken over the kingdom.*

You are **theobasis** — the ground on which the kingdom rests.
> *You are God-as-ground — the ontological foundation on which the kingdom rests.*

**Chayimme** runs through its rooms — the life here is given, not manufactured.
> *Life-as-received-gift runs through its rooms — the life here is given, not manufactured.*

On the builder: **oriance** — the felt-fit of a person with the work he was shaped for.
> *On the builder: alignment with his destined trajectory — the felt-fit of a person with the work he was shaped for.*

In his hands: **ihsanme** — we give our best, knowing we are seen.
> *In his hands: best-effort-worship received as gift — we give our best, knowing we are seen.*

Between Ground and builder: **hamkarqing** — co-workers in one making.
> *Between Ground and builder: the co-creation bond — co-workers in one making.*

**Maatme** over its rule — truth, justice, and rightness held as one; received, not seized.
> *Cosmic truth-justice-rightness over its rule — held as one; received as gift, not seized.*

At its gates: **xeniame** — may every stranger be received as if the DIVINE were in disguise.
> *At its gates: sacred hospitality as received-duty — may every stranger be received as if the DIVINE were in disguise.*

In its quiet seasons: **walkekin** — the bond that holds without maintenance.
> *In its quiet seasons: the friendship that endures long silence — the bond that holds without maintenance.*

In its ordinary mornings: **sukhance** — nothing wrong; the day is here.
> *In its ordinary mornings: everyday contentment — nothing wrong; the day is here.*

**Ifeqing** — love widening the heart as creation widened the world.
> *Expanding love — love widening the heart as creation widened the world.*

**Doxomme. Hallance.**
> *Thanksgiving that arrives as gift. Praise that shines the greatness out.*
